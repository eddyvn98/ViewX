# Vivutrade - High-Performance Trading Chart & Strategy Platform
# Language: English (EN) & Vietnamese (VI)

[PLATFORM OVERVIEW]
Vivutrade is a professional trading workspace designed for speed, precision, and data-driven decision making. It integrates advanced charting with real-time strategy monitoring and performance analytics.
- Name: Vivutrade
- URL: https://vivutrade.io.vn
- Core Markets: Forex, XAUUSD (Gold), Cryptocurrencies, Indices.

[CORE MODULES & CAPABILITIES]
1. Real-time Trading Chart:
   - Ultra-smooth candle rendering using Lightweight Charts.
   - Live data feed with sub-second latency.
   - Specialized for price action and technical analysis.

2. Strategy Matrix Monitor (Signal Scanner):
   - Real-time monitoring of dozens of technical strategies across multiple symbols and timeframes (M1 to Monthly).
   - Visual BUY/SELL signals with Time-to-Live (TTL) tracking.
   - Built-in indicators like RSI, Trend detection, and Volume analysis.

3. Backtest & Performance Dashboard:
   - Detailed trade analytics: Winrate, Profit Factor, Max Drawdown.
   - Equity Curve visualization to track strategy growth over historical data.
   - Interactive trade logs and individual trade analysis.

[TARGET AUDIENCE]
- Scalpers & Intraday Traders looking for high-speed signal detection.
- Quantitative traders building and testing rule-based strategies.
- XAUUSD (Gold) specialized traders using multi-timeframe correlation.

[TECH STACK]
- Frontend: Next.js 15, React 19, Tailwind CSS.
- Charts: Lightweight Charts by TradingView.
- State Management: Zustand (highly optimized for real-time updates).
- UI: Glassmorphism design, Lucide icons, Framer Motion animations.
